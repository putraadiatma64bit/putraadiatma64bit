🛒
Purchasing

Capital is used to buy goods or rent services (cash/credit). Stock increases, capital decreases.

📤
Extract

Goods leave stock without a normal sale: sold at cost price to convert from wholesale to retail and for manufacturing raw materials, or discarded (damaged/expired/waste). Stock decreases.

🏷️
Sales

Goods/services are sold to customers (cash/credit). Revenue comes in, stock decreases, capital increases.

The net result of these three processes is calculated as profit. Part of the profit is distributed as dividend to investors based on the percentage of capital they invested — then the cycle repeats.
